QUICK EDIT — made by sg0hann

Open QuickEdit.exe, click Start, then return to Creative Destruction.
Edit: press G and move to your shape. The app confirms and applies it.
Reset: press G and move to the TOP/default shape. It restores automatically.
Auto reset uses an ON/OFF switch and starts ON. The switch applies immediately.
Keep Select shape only unchecked for automatic apply.

F6: start/pause. F10: exit completely. Esc: cancel.
Close X: hide to the Windows system tray and keep running.
Right-click the tray icon: Open, Start/Pause, or Quit.
Use windowed or borderless mode. No Python installation is needed.
After changing settings: Apply settings, then Start/F6.
360 Hz is a scan target; the actual scan rate is displayed and depends on your PC.
If clicks are missed, try Balanced or a 16 ms click hold.

Idle mode: mouse hook and high-resolution timers are disabled.
Input hooks run on a separate thread from the window.
The window size is locked. Static wallpapers use no animation loop.
The fixed default background is always used; custom wallpapers are disabled.
Settings-applied confirmations are bigger and green.
Green button + green dot mean the helper is ON.
